# Money Map v2.0.0

Released locally on 2026-08-11.

Money Map v2 makes Goals the default return loop. The primary card answers whether the owner
moved closer to the selected goal, what changed between distinct financial observations, and
which deterministic milestone is binding next.

## What changed

- Goals records immutable, source-fingerprinted observations and compares only distinct source
  states. Reopening source-equivalent data does not create a duplicate check-in.
- Comparisons preserve supported components and label any remaining difference as unexplained
  residual instead of inventing a cause.
- One deterministic milestone is selected in order: restore the protected cash floor, repair a
  recurring cash-flow gap, fund the goal at its required pace, or preserve completion.
- Retirement is a separate occasional solvency tool. Operational goals are excluded unless one
  is explicitly selected for a projection.
- Life Lab is a separate experimental workspace. Drafts stay isolated, and a supported value
  crosses into Goals or Retirement only through explicit reviewed promotion.
- Existing payroll, imports, accounts, balance history, forecasts, Life Lab history, exact-decimal
  arithmetic, provenance, and reconciliation evidence remain authoritative.

## Acceptance and recovery

The owner completed the uncoached Goals loop from the initial card in approximately 15 seconds
and identified direction, the distinct financial change, and the binding milestone. The release
was qualified with synthetic gates plus a verified isolated backup/restore and migration drill.

The schema head is `0009_goal_persistence`. No `0010` migration is part of this release.
Downgrading to the pre-v2 revision removes v2-only goal programs, check-ins, components, and their
v2-only edits. The recovery drill proved that pre-v2 tables, including historical Life Lab
evidence, remain intact. Exact private backup and rollback commands are retained only in ignored
local release evidence.

## Known limitations

- Money Map remains a private, local-only application bound to `127.0.0.1`.
- Provider connections are optional and read-only; manual import remains supported.
- The release does not move money, trade, transfer, underwrite a purchase, or provide financial
  advice or probabilistic success scores.
- Merchant budgeting, cloud sync, notifications, hosted deployment, and new providers are outside
  v2.0.0.
