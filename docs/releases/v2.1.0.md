# Money Map v2.1.0

Released locally on 2026-08-11.

Money Map v2.1 makes Cash Flow the default product center. The first screen separates selected-
period history from the current recurring pattern and shows how that recurring pattern affects
the selected goal date.

## What changed

- Cash Flow opens first and reconciles exact period-aware money in, money out, and net cash flow.
  Transfers stay excluded, and partial boundary months remain explicit.
- Historical selected-period net stays separate from the current recurring monthly margin or gap.
  Goal pace and combined monthly improvement retain the accepted `goal-arithmetic-v1` formulas.
- Goal options can explore a different date, one-time reservation, recurring spending reduction,
  after-tax income, or a combination. These drafts are nonpersistent until the owner deliberately
  acts elsewhere; exploring does not edit Goals, reserve money, or move money.
- Goals, Retirement, and Life Lab remain available in a quieter hierarchy. Goals preserves its
  observation and check-in contracts, while Retirement and Life Lab keep their separate planning
  and experimental boundaries.
- Retirement now distinguishes an accessible cash-flow gap from true later exhaustion and retains
  the supported late-life assets evidence instead of describing it as depletion.
- Life Lab keeps unfinished missions selectable, removes misleading zero-value route cards in the
  funded-only state, and states its 51-whole-month convention separately from Goals' fractional-
  month convention.
- Touch-layout controls, including focused dialogs, now retain a minimum 44-pixel target.

## Schema, privacy, and recovery

The schema head remains `0009_goal_persistence`; this release adds no migration. Money Map remains
local-only and binds to `127.0.0.1`. Plaid is optional and read-only, manual imports remain
supported, and the application does not move money. Release qualification used an isolated
verified backup and disposable restored copy; restoring that verified `0009` backup replaces a
failed disposable copy without provider sync, import, payroll regeneration, or invented SQL.

## Known limitations

- Cash Flow and goal-gap results depend on the completeness and freshness of locally available
  source evidence; unavailable or incomplete states remain explicit.
- Projections and scenario arithmetic are deterministic planning evidence, not probabilities,
  financial recommendations, tax advice, or purchase underwriting.
- Merchant budgeting, cloud sync, notifications, hosted deployment, new providers, and automated
  financial action remain outside Money Map v2.1.0.
